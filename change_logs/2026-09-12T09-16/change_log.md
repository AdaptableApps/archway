# What changed in Archway — product features and the new store

Everything below is live on the beta environment. These are the changes you can **see or feel** while using
the app.

The theme of this release is **telling customers what they are buying**. A product can now be described in
detail — the plans it is sold under, the features it offers, and exactly what each plan includes — and the
store page has been rebuilt to show it.

> ### 👉 [https://adaptableapps.demo.us.app.archwayportal.com](https://adaptableapps.demo.us.app.archwayportal.com)

**New** — capability that was not there before &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Improved** — existing behaviour made better
&nbsp;·&nbsp; **Fixed** — something that was wrong &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Changed** — deliberately different, and worth knowing.

---

## Describing a product

- **New** — A product can now be sold under several **Plans**, each with its own prices.
- **New** — A product can list the **Features** it offers, and the individual details under each feature.
- **New** — Every plan can say what it includes for each of those details — a number, a limit, or a short phrase like "Unlimited".
- **New** — Features, plans and their details each have their own screens, so they can be set up and edited directly.

## The store

- **New** — The store is now laid out plan by plan: each plan shows its own prices, followed by everything it includes.
- **New** — Plans can be marked as most popular, and are highlighted on the page.
- **Changed** — What a plan includes is shown as the value itself, in green — "Up to 3", "500" — rather than a tick you had to interpret.
- **Changed** — Plans sold by conversation show **Talk to sales for pricing**, with no price and no Add to Cart.
- **Changed** — A plan with no price set up is no longer listed; a heading standing over an empty space read as broken.
- **Improved** — Each feature detail can carry a description, revealed by expanding the row.

## Setting products up

- **Improved** — Adding a price, plan, feature or detail fills in the product you are adding it to, instead of asking for it again.
- **Improved** — Lists of features and plans show the display name you gave them, not the internal one.
- **Improved** — Dropdowns on the product screens offer only the options belonging to the product you are working on.
- **Improved** — Fields on the product screens are in a sensible order, with the on/off switches grouped together at the end.

## Fixes along the way

- **Fixed** — The app could sit on a spinner forever after opening a newly created tenancy, and now asks for a region instead.
- **Fixed** — A session left over from earlier could sign you out and leave you stuck, needing the app restarted. It now recovers on its own.
- **Fixed** — The app could flicker between the home page and the store without ever showing either, hiding the message explaining why.
- **Fixed** — Dropdowns offered "Empty" as though it were a real choice; they now show nothing until you pick one, and will not accept it as an answer.
- **Fixed** — Leaving a number field empty no longer causes an error.
- **Improved** — Sign in is offered on the sign-up page, so you can switch without going back.

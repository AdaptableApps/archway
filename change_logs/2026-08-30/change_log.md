# What changed in Archway — 24 to 30 August 2026

Everything below is live on the beta environment. These are the changes you can **see or feel** while using
the app; the first of them landed on the Wednesday.

> ### 👉 [https://adaptableapps.demo.us.app.archwayportal.com](https://adaptableapps.demo.us.app.archwayportal.com)

**New** — capability that was not there before &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Improved** — existing behaviour made better
&nbsp;·&nbsp; **Fixed** — something that was wrong.

---

## Wednesday 26 August — subscriptions and billing

- **Fixed** — Buying a subscription now takes payment straight away and activates immediately.
- **Fixed** — Subscriptions show their real status, instead of reporting "Cancelled" whenever anything went wrong.
- **Fixed** — The inactive-tenancy page is no longer a dead end; you can navigate away from it.
- **Improved** — My Subscriptions loads far faster: it no longer re-checks every subscription with the payment provider on each visit.
- **Improved** — A new tenancy now names each of its workspaces after the region it serves.
- **Improved** — Extra breathing room at the bottom of the subscription view, so the last row is no longer against the edge.
- **Improved** — Two product prices were retired from the catalogue.

## Friday 28 August — navigation moves to the app bar

- **New** — Navigation has moved out of the side menu and into the app bar at the top of every screen.
- **Fixed** — A failed sign-in now shows a clear message instead of leaving the app unresponsive.

## Saturday 29 August — forms and actions

- **New** — You are warned before leaving a page while adding or editing a record, so work is no longer lost by accident.
- **New** — Tenant admins now get a Tenant Center button on their store while it has no products set up yet.
- **Fixed** — Every action now appears in the app-bar menu; it could previously open with nothing in it.
- **Fixed** — Actions that have been switched off no longer appear on screen.
- **Improved** — Dialogs open with the first field already focused, so you can start typing immediately.

## Sunday 30 August — app bar, pickers and the store

- **New** — Tenant Center and Profile are now reachable directly from the app bar.
- **Fixed** — The app no longer occasionally sits on a spinner and never finishes loading.
- **Fixed** — Sign In and Sign Up are only offered on a store when that store is actually open for business.
- **Improved** — The app-bar menu lists every available action, and is hidden on screens where it does not apply.
- **Improved** — The app-bar menu is easier to read, with a clearer background and a highlight as you move down it.
- **Improved** — Fields that offer a list now show a dropdown arrow and read "Search here or select a value", rather than looking like a plain search box.
